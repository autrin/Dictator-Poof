# Name and branding

**Dictator Poof** is the name of Autrin Hakimi's project. Autrin is the founder and
director of the official project. The GPL license covers original source code;
it does not grant trademark rights in the project name or branding.
This policy does not change the copyright license for artwork or other files.

You may say that a fork is "based on Dictator Poof" and link to the upstream
repository. Use a distinct name and branding for a fork to avoid confusion
with the official project. Do not imply endorsement or call a fork an official
release unless Autrin has approved that wording. Accurate descriptive references
to the original project are welcome. This policy does not impose a blanket
renaming requirement as a condition of the GPL or restrict uses permitted by law.

A separate name does not remove the attribution requirement. Distributions
containing original project material must preserve credit for Autrin Hakimi
and contributors under the GPLv3 section 7(b) term in [NOTICE](NOTICE).
Credit your own changes separately; do not imply that you authored the
retained original material or that Autrin endorses your release.

This policy is intended to make attribution clear while welcoming forks,
experiments, and pull requests. It is a project policy, not a replacement for
legal trademark registration or legal advice.
