# Name and branding

**Dictator Poof** is the name of Autrin Hakimi's project. Autrin is the founder and
director of the official project. The GPL license covers original source code;
it does not grant permission to use the project name, logo, official artwork,
or other branding in a way that implies endorsement.

You may say that a fork is "based on Dictator Poof" and link to the upstream
repository. A modified distribution must use a distinct name and branding,
identify its changes, preserve the copyright and license notices, and keep
the founder credit in the accompanying notice. Do not call a fork an official
release unless Autrin has approved that wording.

This policy is intended to make attribution clear while welcoming forks,
experiments, and pull requests. It is a project policy, not a replacement for
legal trademark registration or legal advice.
