# Nowruz album prints

`nowruz_prints.png` contains the three illustrations used on the title
screen and in the family album. The artwork was generated for Dictator Poof
with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool. The project distributes the
asset under [GPL-3.0-or-later](../../LICENSE).

## Art prompt

A single horizontal panoramic triptych of three equal-width illustrated album prints, edge to edge, no gutters, borders, letters or numbers. Left third: a contemporary Iranian family home on Nowruz morning, fresh sabzeh wheatgrass tied with a scarlet ribbon, a small mirror reflecting a sunlit garden and three cypresses, painted eggs on a Persian woven tablecloth. Middle third: close-up of a Tehran neighborhood pastry counter, a cream puff, pale rice cookies dusted with poppy seeds and tiny four-leaf chickpea cookies in a white cardboard gift box, brass tongs and green tile behind. Right third: Grandma's amber tea in a slender clear waist-shaped glass on a saucer, walnuts and a worn red patterned Persian rug, warm evening lamplight. Beautiful hand-pulled multilayer linocut and gouache illustration, textured warm uncoated paper, dark petrol green, dusty saffron, persimmon, pale sage and cream. Each still life self-contained, large focal silhouette centered in its third. Quiet, intimate domestic Iranian life. No people, no flags, no text, no watermark. Landscape 3:1.
